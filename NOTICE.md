## Modifications and Maintenance

This fork is independently maintained by ZHIXIANGMO.

The project introduces an orange-haired pixel-art character system, expanded article illustration workflows, WeChat cover generation, Python image validation and composition utilities, and additional documentation and quality guidelines.

These modifications focus on making AI-assisted visual content creation more consistent, practical, and reusable for Chinese-speaking creators.
